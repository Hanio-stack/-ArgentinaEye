# ArgentinaEye Learning Guide

ArgentinaEye is primarily a learning tool. The goal is not to produce a single "buy / don't buy" number, but to learn how macro conditions, company operations, valuation, and risk connect.

## The five layers

### 1. Country thesis

Ask whether Argentina is becoming a more investable economy.

Watch:
- Milei political durability / 2027 election
- monthly inflation
- BCRA reserves
- primary fiscal balance
- RIGI capital commitments

Beginner question: **Is the country becoming more stable, or only looking better temporarily?**

### 2. Industry thesis

Ask whether the industry is actually expanding.

For ArgentinaEye:
- Vaca Muerta oil and gas output
- pipeline and processing capacity
- LNG / export infrastructure
- electricity demand and market reform

Beginner question: **Even if Argentina improves, does this specific industry get more customers and more volume?**

### 3. Company thesis

Understand what each company sells.

- VIST: upstream oil and gas production — "dig"
- PAM: diversified energy — "integrated"
- CEPU: power generation — "make electricity"
- TGS: gas transport and processing — "move it"

Beginner question: **What exact event makes this company's profit rise?**

### 4. Valuation

A good company can still be a bad purchase if expectations are already very high.

Useful measures:
- PER: price compared with earnings
- EBITDA: operating earning power before interest/tax/depreciation/amortization
- EV/EBITDA: whole-company price compared with EBITDA
- FCF: cash left after running and investing in the business
- Net debt: debt minus cash

A simplified valuation bridge:

`EBITDA × EV/EBITDA multiple = enterprise value`

`enterprise value − net debt = equity value`

`equity value ÷ share count = rough value per share`

This is a model, not a prediction.

### 5. Scenario and position size

Build three scenarios:
- Bear: things go worse than expected
- Base: reasonable central case
- Bull: things go substantially better

Then ask:
- What has to happen for each case?
- How much could the share price fall?
- How much could it rise?
- What evidence would make me change my mind?

For a learning-sized position, the purpose is to observe how the thesis changes in the real world, not to maximize short-term profit.

## Thesis score is not valuation score

ArgentinaEye's current company scores measure **thesis health**. They do not say the stock is cheap.

Example:
- Thesis health 85/100
- Valuation extremely expensive

This can still be a poor entry price.

The planned valuation panel should stay separate from thesis health so the UI teaches this distinction.

## NISA note (checked 2026-09-03)

VIST, PAM, CEPU and TGS are US-listed ADRs handled by SBI Securities and Matsui Securities. They can be bought in the NISA Growth Investment Framework (成長投資枠), subject to broker-side final order checks.

Important beginner detail: US stocks are normally bought in whole-share units at these brokers. A monthly budget can therefore be smaller than the price of one share. "Amount-specified" US stock accumulation at SBI still buys whole shares within the budget; it does not create fractional shares.

## Before buying: five questions

1. Can I explain how the company makes money in one sentence?
2. Can I name the two or three variables that drive profit?
3. Can I name the event that would break my thesis?
4. Have I checked whether the growth is already reflected in valuation?
5. If the stock falls 50%, do I know what data would tell me whether to hold, buy more, or exit?
